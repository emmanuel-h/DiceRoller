Roll any combination of dice for all your games.

THE DICE

• D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 and D20 built in.
• 12 colours to choose from.
• Up to 20 dice of each type in a single roll.
• Up to 3 custom dice, from 2 to 1000 faces (D7, D30, D100…).

Tap the right half of a die to add one, the left half to remove one.

SHAKE TO ROLL

Shake your phone to roll the dice you picked. The sensitivity is adjustable, and the feature can be turned off.

HISTORY

Your last 50 rolls are kept: the dice rolled, the values that came up, and when.

PRIVACY

• No account.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
• No permissions and no internet access: your data stays on your phone.

OTHER

• Light or dark theme.
• In English and French.
• Phones and tablets, Android 7.0 or later.
