A dice bag that lives in your pocket and never loses a die under the sofa.

Tap the dice you need, press Roll, and read the result. That is all there is: a tray of dice, one button, and the total. No sign-up, no board to set up, no tutorial to sit through.

THE WHOLE SET

D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 and D20, hand-painted, in twelve colours — amethyst, sapphire, ruby, jade, gold and more. Tap the right half of a die to add one, the left half to take one away, up to twenty of each. Need something stranger? Make up to three custom dice of any size from 2 to 1000 faces: a D7, a D30, a D100.

A ROLL YOU CAN READ

The dice tumble and land, and each one shows its value right on its face. Matching values are grouped, so five sixes read as one die and ×5, and the total sits underneath. A clatter of dice plays as they fall; turn it off in the settings if the table is quiet.

SHAKE TO ROLL

Pick your dice, then shake your phone like a cup. Set how hard a shake it takes, or switch it off altogether.

A MEMORY FOR DISPUTES

The last fifty rolls are kept in a log under the result — what was rolled, what came up, and when — so "what did you roll for initiative?" has an answer.

WHAT ISN'T HERE

No account. No ads, no trackers, no analytics. No permissions at all, and no internet access: the app could not send your rolls anywhere even if it wanted to.

Parchment by day, leather by candlelight. English and French. Works on phones and tablets, Android 7.0 and later.
