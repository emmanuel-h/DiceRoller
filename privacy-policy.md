# Privacy Policy for Dice Roller

**Effective Date:** October 2026

## 1. Overview

Dice Roller is a simple dice-rolling application for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## 2. What Data We Collect

**We collect no personal data.**

Specifically, Dice Roller:
- Does not require an account or login
- Does not collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Does not track your location, device ID, or advertising ID
- Does not integrate with analytics or crash-reporting services
- Does not contain advertising
- Makes no network requests of any kind

## 3. How Data Is Stored

All app data — your chosen dice colour, language and theme, your custom dice, your sound and shake-to-roll settings, and the log of your recent rolls — is stored **locally on your device only**, in private storage that other apps cannot read. This data:

- Is never sent to us or to any server we control
- Is never shared with third parties
- Is never sold or used for marketing
- Remains entirely under your control

You can delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings.

### Android Auto Backup

Dice Roller uses Android's standard Auto Backup feature, which is provided by the operating system rather than by us. If you have device backup enabled in your Android settings, your device may copy the app's settings to **your own personal Google Drive account**, where they are used to restore them when you reinstall the app or set up a new device.

We have no access to these backups. They belong to your Google account and are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can disable this at any time in your device's backup settings.

## 4. Permissions and Sensors

Dice Roller requests **no permissions**.

The optional *Shake to roll* feature reads the device's accelerometer, which Android makes available without a permission, and only while the app is open on screen. The readings are used on the spot to detect a shake and are never stored or transmitted. You can turn the feature off in the app's settings.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Dice Roller does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app contains no embedded trackers, SDKs, or external APIs that collect information about you or your usage.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Dice Roller is suitable for users of all ages. Since we collect no data whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or the digital age of consent in your jurisdiction).

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon publication. Your continued use of Dice Roller constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
